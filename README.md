## 🌌 About Me

I am a **Data Science Student** at **RP Institute (Affiliated to the University of Mumbai)** operating at the intersection of **statistical computing, applied epidemiology, and modern web architecture**.

My work bridges the gap between raw, messy real-world datasets and intuitive digital experiences. Whether mathematically decomposing multi-year government disease surveillance (NCDC/IDSP) through non-parametric rank concordance (**Kendall's $W$**) or engineering low-latency interactive visualizations with **Next.js 16 (Turbopack)** and **TypeScript**, I build end-to-end systems with high rigor and aesthetic precision.

## 🌟 Flagship Projects & Research

### 🌊 [Seasonal Disease Patterns in Maharashtra (Field-Project)](https://github.com/RizzyCoder19/Field-Project)
> **Official University of Mumbai Field Research Monograph & Web Platform**
* **Scope:** 237 weekly government surveillance bulletins (2022–2026), 809 verified outbreak records, 20,955 baseline cases, and 266 deaths across 36 districts.
* **Methods:** Non-parametric rank concordance (**Kendall's $W$**), 3-month concentration ratios (**CR3**), outlier impact simulation, and clinical field immersion in Palghar District (PHC Nallasopara West).
* **Live Web Portal:** [fieldprojects.vercel.app](https://fieldprojects.vercel.app)
* **Tech:** Next.js 16 (App Router), Turbopack, TypeScript, Tailwind, Python.

### 📐 [DAA-Research (Design & Analysis of Algorithms)](https://github.com/RizzyCoder19/DAA-Research)
> **Empirical Algorithmic Benchmarking & Complexity Analysis**
* Comparative runtime, space complexity, and empirical asymptotic verification for advanced algorithmic paradigms.
* **Tech:** TypeScript, Python, Benchmarking harnesses.

### 🏛️ [college_erp](https://github.com/RizzyCoder19/college_erp)
> **Modular Academic & Administrative Enterprise Architecture**
* Full-stack campus resource planning, student attendance tracking, and examination result processing system.
